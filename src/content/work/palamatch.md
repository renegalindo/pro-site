Palamatch is the easiest way to find the perfect padel racket online. I took it from an  early idea to shipped product in under two months. It was the first project where I designed and built the end to end, from defining the feature set, to wireframes, to the frontend code that launched to production. As with [Cabify](/work/cabify) and [Invopop](/work/invopop), I collaborated with Berta Porta on the brand design.
