Palamatch is the easiest way to find the perfect padel racket online. I took it from idea to shipped product in under two months. It was the first project I designed and built end to end, from defining the feature set, to early wireframes, to the frontend code that's live today. As with [Cabify](/work/cabify) and [Invopop](/work/invopop), I collaborated with Berta Porta on brand design.
