Cabify is one of the largest ride-hailing companies in Spain and Latin America. I joined as the second product designer and led design for the rider app and the purple rebrand. I also helped hire and onboard the team as it grew from 2 to 8 designers, before moving to the new business division, where I worked on [Movo](/work/movo) and [Lana](/work/lana).
